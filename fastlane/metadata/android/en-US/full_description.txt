Kal-Track is a simple, fast calorie and macro counter that respects your privacy.

SNAP IT, LOG IT
• Take a photo of your plate: artificial intelligence recognizes the foods, estimates portions and calculates calories, protein, carbs, fat and sugar.
• Describe a meal in a few words ("spaghetti bolognese and salad") when you have no photo.
• Scan a barcode or a product's nutrition label.
• Re-add your recent and favorite meals in one tap.
• Review and edit every item before saving.

A CLEAR DIARY
• Calorie goal calculated from your profile (basal metabolic rate and daily energy expenditure) to lose, maintain or gain weight.
• Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks, with your daily distribution.
• Calorie ring, macro bars, sugar and hydration.
• Log meals on past days.

INSIGHTS AND WEIGHT
• Daily, weekly and monthly charts with your goal lines.
• Weight curve and trend over 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year or all time.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• No account, no ads, no trackers.
• Profile, meals, photos and weigh-ins are stored only on your phone.
• Export and import backups at any time.
• Only the AI analyses you request and barcode lookups use the internet.

CONVENIENT
• AI analyses included for free, within a daily limit.
• 6 languages: English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian.
• Metric or imperial units (kg/lb, cm/ft-in, ml/fl oz).
• Light, dark or automatic theme.
• Works offline (except AI and barcode lookups).

Kal-Track is intended for adults (18+). Nutrition values are estimates and do not replace the advice of a doctor or dietitian.
